A knowledge note holds something you worked out once and do not want to work out
again. Give it a title you would search for, not a title that describes where it
came from.

Links between notes are how the graph gets built. This one points at
[[TOOLS/example-reference-note]], and `developer-os brain lint` will tell you
if a link stops resolving.

Delete these four example notes whenever you like — nothing depends on them.
