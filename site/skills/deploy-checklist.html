deploy-checklist

Produces a deployment checklist specific to the repository, with a GO/NO-GO verdict before the deploy and checks after it, covering validation, migrations, feature flags, monitoring, communication and rollback.

Group
Engineering
Kind
Action
Writes
Nothing
Asks before
Sending any communication: it only drafts it

When it runs

When you plan a deployment, a release to production or another shipping event:

  • can we deploy to production?
  • wdrażamy na produkcję
  • checklista deployu
  • czy możemy deployować

What it does

An item whose evidence is unavailable (no CI status, no dashboard access) is marked UNVERIFIED, never assumed to pass.

  1. Validation. It detects the package manager from the lockfile and runs the repository's real test, lint, type-check and build commands, and requires green CI on the intended release branch.
  2. Migrations. It checks that they ran locally or on staging, are backward compatible, have a backup, an expected lock time and runtime, and a tested rollback.
  3. Feature flags. It checks defaults and rollout percentages for risky behavior.
  4. Monitoring. It confirms dashboards, error tracking, alerts and explicit abort thresholds.
  5. Communication. It drafts deployment, client or status messages when needed, without sending them.
  6. Rollback. It writes the exact rollback command, the recovery steps and the metrics that trigger it. Provider-specific commands come from the repository's configuration; if the target is unknown, it asks.
  7. Window and owner. It confirms a suitable deployment window and an owner available for at least an hour of monitoring.
  8. Verdict. GO, or NO-GO when any item above failed or is UNVERIFIED, with what would turn it into GO.
  9. After the deploy. It monitors error rate and latency against the abort thresholds, inspects new exceptions, runs a smoke test of the critical flow, and records the result: stable, or rolled back with the trigger. It offers brain-capture when the deployment taught a lasting lesson.

Boundaries

Stops when

Before the deployment, at the GO/NO-GO verdict; after it, at the recorded result. You run the deployment.

Never

  • Deploy, run a production migration, or change a feature flag.
  • Send deployment, client or status communication without your explicit authorization.
  • Mark an item passed without evidence.
  • Invent provider-specific commands.
