## Context

A knowledge note holds something you worked out once and do not want to work out again.

## Decision

Give a note a title you would search for, not a title that describes where it came from.

- One topic per note. Two topics make two notes that link to each other.
- The `summary` line is what a search result shows; write it so it stands on its own.
- Write down the why. The what is usually in the code; the reason almost never is.

## Why

Search results and Bases tables show the title and the summary. A note that does not answer a
question from those two lines gets skipped, then rewritten.
